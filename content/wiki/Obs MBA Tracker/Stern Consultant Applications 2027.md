> [!warning] I have a hacky setup, below is not streamlined for common-use

I have a CRM in obsidian for companies. Each company I'm applying to gets a note, stored under 'Calendar/2027 Applications/'. All the details of the applications are kept in it, with branch-off tasknote files per item. Eg, if a company has an application, assessment, and other submissions, they each get a note.

I also have a very hacky [[TaskNotes Gantt Chart]] that I will share eventually which helps me visualize the process!

![[Blog/static/images/posts/Pasted image 20261117142416.png]]

Mostly, I use a modified Obsidian base that looks in the folder 'Calendar/2027 Applications/', identifies which company has a tag like 'consulting'. Tweaks to the hacky plugin means that it then organizes those tasks by the relevant company.
